## 2. Services and solutions

Typical jobs include support, appointments, qualification, reminders, logistics and embedded voice products. Its older visual Workflows product is retired; Squads are a distinct orchestration model. [Catalog and limits](./competitor-vapi-audit-2026-10-24.md).

**Retell:** inbound/outbound voice and chat/SMS, prompt and conversation-flow builders, managed/BYO telephony, batch calling, transfer, IVR/DTMF, booking, CRM/helpdesk functions/MCP, synchronized knowledge, contact memory, pre/post conversation workflows and a building/debugging assistant. Production services include immutable versions/tags, A/B routing, simulations, extraction, AI QA, live listening/takeover, alerts, privacy/retention, prepaid billing and enterprise/partner delivery. Industry positioning includes healthcare, insurance, finance, logistics, retail, travel and collections. [Catalog and limits](./competitor-retell-audit-2026-10-24.md).

**Outpero:** instant lead calls, bulk campaigns, inbound receptionist, documented voice broadcast, seven primary Indian-market languages, script/training tools, lead capture/pipeline, caller memory/callbacks, Calendar, WhatsApp and custom HTTP actions, recordings/analytics and a call/campaign API. The separate agency publishes 19 services: WhatsApp automation; lead qualification; booking; follow-up sequences; cart recovery; invoice/payment reminders; internal/custom automation; inbound/outbound/custom voice agents; business websites; landing pages; redesign; website plus lead pipeline; e-commerce/custom websites; automation audits; conversion/website audits. ## 4. Implementation choices customers receive

**Vapi** owns hosted conversational orchestration. Customers can configure platform providers or BYOK, custom compatible model/speech endpoints, retrieval, artifact storage, SIP/carriers, REST/SDKs, widgets and webhooks. Customer keys or buckets do not make the whole system self-hosted. [Data flow](https://docs.vapi.ai/security-and-privacy/data-flow).

**Retell** provides a managed stack with SDK/API/widget/SIP integration. Its custom-LLM mode opens a customer response WebSocket while Retell still handles telephony, transcription, turn-taking and speech synthesis. Custom LLM has feature tradeoffs, including loss of built-in simulations. Universal STT/LLM/TTS BYOK was not established. [Custom LLM](https://docs.retellai.com/integrate-llm/overview).

**Outpero** packages job templates and managed telephony around a documented streaming speech/model pipeline. Its external boundaries are lead-intake HTTP, REST call/campaign APIs, custom actions, Calendar and customer-owned WhatsApp providers. Many advertised CRM/automation integrations are HTTP recipes, not native connectors. Its agency adds customer-specific workflows and websites. Private backend topology and exact active model routing remain unknown. [Integrations](https://outpero.com/ai-voice-agents/features/lead-intake-api-and-integrations).

These explain supported integration contracts. They do not reveal private database schemas, tenant isolation, queue durability, encryption-key operations, deployment topology or source code.

## 5. Constraints that matter in buying or copying features

- Vapi API Request tools do not automatically retry failures/timeouts as users might assume. Published snapshots can still follow Latest tool revisions. New-account chat has payment prerequisites, SMS has Twilio/customer-initiated restrictions, and enterprise entitlement documentation contains inconsistencies.
- Retell simulations can call real external tools, particularly MCP; custom LLM loses several testing features. Browser-test pricing and some role/plan descriptions conflict across official pages.
- Outpero Calendar is documented as IST-only booking, without listing availability/rescheduling/cancellation. WhatsApp is approved-template sending through customer providers, not a full reply-handling inbox. Custom actions have documented one-attempt/10-second bounds.
- Outpero account webhooks promise retries up to a day, while employee post-call docs describe one attempt. Keep the two contracts separate.
- Neither a configuration switch nor a marketing compliance badge demonstrates runtime enforcement. No independent audio/latency/recovery comparison was performed.

Every detailed report lists unresolved claims and direct official sources.

## 6. Implications for our platform

1. Choose the first buyer and job. An inbound receptionist that answers correctly and delivers a confirmed callback is a tighter launch scope than a generic all-features platform.
2. Fix trust boundaries first: SSRF/egress, actual privacy/access/retention enforcement, authoritative evaluations and atomic deployment.
3. Make execution durable: call events, workflow triggers, webhook deliveries, end-state reconciliation and usage accounting.
4. Enforce concurrency, duration, silence, budgets and rate limits. Then expose tested cost/quality presets with real measured traces.
5. Package guided onboarding: business facts/hours/language, agent, safe test, number, readiness, real call, saved business outcome.
6. Productize callback notification/ownership and one calendar integration. Add contacts, structured outcomes and conversion reporting around them.
7. Keep our intended strengths: immutable environment releases, revision-bound governed tools, explicit sandbox modes and provider neutrality. Verify these claims with tests before marketing them.
8. Add transfers/outbound/campaigns only with market need and complete consent/DNC/windows/retries/capacity controls.
9. Keep billing explicit: AI/voice/carrier/number costs, rounding, trial usage, taxes, optional support and bespoke implementation fees.

This prioritization is our analysis based on the competitor research and preceding local code audit, not a vendor-endorsed recommendation.
